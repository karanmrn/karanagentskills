Breaking the Kill Chain

// defense-in-depth checklist
[✓]Enforce phishing-resistant MFA FIDO2 / passkeys on every account, no SMS fallback
[✓]Patch internet-facing systems fast edge devices & known-exploited CVEs within 72h
[✓]Segment the network kill the flat LAN — contain lateral movement by design
[✓]Least-privilege & just-in-time access no standing domain admin; tier your identities
[✓]Centralize logging + EDR you can't respond to what you can't see — 24/7 detection
[✓]Encrypt & back up offline immutable, tested restores defeat double-extortion
[✓]Rehearse the incident-response plan tabletop quarterly — response time is money
[✓]Assume breach: monitor egress DLP + anomaly detection on outbound data flows
