# Style presets

Pick ONE and commit to it fully. The goal is a deck that looks intentionally
designed, not defaulted. Each preset lists a font pairing (load via Google Fonts
or Fontshare — inline the `<link>`), a palette, and a mood. Never ship Inter /
Arial / Helvetica as the headline face.

## 1. Editorial Serif (default in the template)
- **Fonts:** Fraunces (display) + Newsreader (body)
- **Palette:** ink `#f4f1ea`, bg `#0b1020`→`#131a33`, accent `#ffcc4d`
- **Mood:** confident, literary, keynote-grade. Large headlines, generous margins.

## 2. Bold Signal
- **Fonts:** Space Grotesk (display) + Inter Tight (body)
- **Palette:** bg `#0a0a0a`, ink `#ffffff`, accent `#ff4d3d`
- **Mood:** high-contrast, punchy. Oversized numbers, tight tracking, flat color blocks.

## 3. Warm Paper
- **Fonts:** Instrument Serif (display) + Work Sans (body)
- **Palette:** bg `#f3ede2`, ink `#1f1b16`, accent `#c2542a`
- **Mood:** calm, tactile, light. Fine hairline rules, muted earth tones.

## 4. Electric Studio
- **Fonts:** Clash Display (display) + Satoshi (body)
- **Palette:** bg `#050814`, ink `#e8ecff`, accent `#6df1c8` / `#a06bff`
- **Mood:** techy, gradient glows, thin grid lines, neon accents on near-black.

## 5. Notebook
- **Fonts:** Gelica / Zodiak (display) + Supreme (body)
- **Palette:** bg `#fbfbf9`, ink `#111`, accent per-section tabs
- **Mood:** structured, tab-based sections, generous whitespace, editorial grids.

## Rules
- Choose atmospheric backgrounds (subtle gradients / grain), not flat white unless the style is deliberately minimal.
- One accent color, used sparingly, for emphasis.
- Keep the type scale on a ratio (e.g. 108 / 72 / 40 / 28 px at 1080p).
- Low-density: 1 idea + ≤3 bullets per slide. High-density: self-contained grids/tables, 4–8 items when legible.
