How the video editor works
▶
Play & scrubUse the transport bar to play/pause (or press space), drag the scrubber to move through the timeline, and change the speed to inspect a moment in slow motion.
💬
Comment for the agentSingle-click any element to comment on it — it pauses and stamps the current timecode, so the agent knows exactly when you mean. Comments collect below; hit Send.
✎
Edit text in placeDouble-click any text on the canvas, type, then press Enter (Esc to cancel). Changes save straight to the file.
↺
ReplayThe video plays through once and stops on the final frame. The transport button becomes Replay — click it (or space) to watch again from the start.
✓
Track your requestsEach sent request shows a badge: Queued → Working (spinner) → Done (✓). The bar below shows when the agent is Listening or Working.
⏻
ContinueWhen the video is finished, hit Continue — the agent hands over anything queued, stops listening, and moves on to the next step. Re-open the editor anytime to keep going.
Close the editor?

This finishes the current stage and tells the agent to stop listening for changes. Anything still in the queue will be handed over first. You can re-open the editor anytime to keep working.
